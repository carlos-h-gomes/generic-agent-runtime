## 9. AI agent rules

- Follow AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md.
- Start with the strict task-triage JSON.
- Follow Clean Code, SOLID, separation of concerns and secure-by-design defaults.
- Prefer existing project patterns over new abstractions.
- Do not use deprecated or unnecessarily complex dependencies if native/simple options solve the problem.
- Keep context lean, but document important context in docs/ai.
- Never rely on mental notes.
- Do not send implementation to human review while tests/lint are known to fail unless the reflection loop has reached its configured limit.

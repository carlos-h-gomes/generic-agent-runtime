Status: uninitialized. Required for a production web or agentic release.

Define detection signals, severity/owner, containment and kill switch, credential revocation and rotation order from a clean, trusted control plane separate from the suspected host, evidence preservation, immutable rebuild, restore verification, communication, monitoring window, post-incident review, and recurrence-prevention ownership.

For a possible personal-data incident, name the controller/privacy decision owner; record when the controller learned that personal data was affected; identify categories and approximate scale without copying records here; assess relevant risk or harm; preserve the decision and communication evidence; and recheck applicable regulator, data-subject, contractual, sector, and jurisdiction deadlines. Never store secrets, customer data, unrestricted logs, or raw forensic artifacts here.
